Scope: the classroom node graph, Octria's hero surface (Flowspace), as a prototype on sample data. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: students, teams and individuals opening a classroom to find their next step and see who is blocking whom. Must not feel overwhelming at first, or gamified or childish. Chrome uses one fluid, Apple-style look on every platform, still honouring Android back and system settings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your work is a course overprinted on the team's terrain, so your next step is the one thing you can't miss. Refuses the rounded-cards-and-grey-connectors board every planning tool ships.

OWN-WORLD: A still ambient ground with floating Apple-style glass chrome (pinned by the user over the earlier opaque bars and contours), faint translucent phase bands, quiet ink for other people, one magenta overprint reserved for the viewer's route. Status keeps map colours: blue, green, grey, with red for blocked. Standard Apple-style controls on translucent chrome, one workhorse sans, tabular dates.

STORY: The viewer opens the classroom, sees their ringed next step, follows the route, marks it done, watches the arrows it unblocks light up, then explores the team by zooming.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left sidebar, toolbar with Graph/Timeline/List and Focus on me. Canvas takes the rest: phase columns left to right, member lanes with the viewer on top, route overprinted, next step ringed and named in a floating card at top right. Minimap bottom right. On phones the sidebar leaves and a bottom bar holds the views.

FORM: Overprint Course, position 7 on the ordered list, seed:7120b70cf524f8a9. Signature move: the magenta route. Raises: one continuous zoom axis (Type Specimen); destructive actions isolated by empty space (Developer Console); focus lifts your lane while the rest dims (Streaming Wall); every status is a drawn mark before a colour (Reference Edition).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Unresolved

Whether real blur holds up on low-end phones. Permissions are not enforced in the prototype.

## Brief-earned exceptions

Progress rings at the far zoom: the planning document (https://claude.ai/artifact/9AjHJVH12FveWmRvfMVKnW, Node graph view, three zoom levels) says "Far: phases as large cards with a progress ring each." They stay thin and single-ink so they don't read as rewards.

Step rectangles: the user asked for bigger rectangles in place of the small circles. This overrides the THESIS line refusing a board of rounded cards; the route, terrain and lanes now carry the identity.
